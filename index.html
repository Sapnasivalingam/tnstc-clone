Chennai

Chennai, formerly known as Madras, is the capital city of Tamil Nadu, the southern most state of India. It is the state's primary city and is located on the Coromandel Coast of the Bay of Bengal.

Bengaluru

Bengaluru is the capital of India's southern Karnataka state. The center of India's high-tech industry, the city is also known for its parks and nightlife.

Kumbakonam

Kumbakonam is a city municipal corporation in the Thanjavur district in the Indian state of Tamil Nadu. It is located 40 km from Thanjavur and 282 km from Chennai.

Tirupati

Tirupati is a city in the Indian state of Andhra Pradesh. The city is home to the important Hindu shrine of Tirumala Venkateshwara Temple.

Thiruvannamalai

Tiruvannamalai is a city and the administrative headquarters of Tiruvannamalai District. It is a Hindu pilgrimage center, famous for the Arulmigu Annamalaiyar Temple and Arunachala Hill.

Madurai is a historic Hindu temple city located on the southern bank of the Vaigai River in Tamil Nadu, India. It is dedicated to the goddess Meenakshi.
